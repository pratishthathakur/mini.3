    • Ingredients

    • Maggi noodles
    • Water
    • Maggi masala
    • Vegetables

    Instructions

    1. Boil water in a pan.
    2. Add noodles and masala.
    3. Add vegetables.
    4. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
    5. Serve hot.

    Maggi is a quick and tasty snack.

    Tip: Add your favourite vegetables.
